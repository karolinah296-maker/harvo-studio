COOPERATION PROCESS

What does the process look like? cooperation?

From the first conversation to a consistent brand image and a finished website.

01
Strategy

Brand conversation and analysis

I get to know the company, offer, clients and project goals. We check which elements are already ready and what needs to be created from scratch. On this basis, a plan tailored to the recipients you want to acquire is created.

02
UI / UX

Graphic design

I determine the style, colors, typography and way of presenting the brand. If the project requires it, I also develop a logo and the basics of identification. Then I create views of the website for desktop and phone.

03
Content

Content and materials

I organize the website structure, write texts and determine the necessary materials. Together we plan photos, graphics, animations and videos so that all elements speak one language.

04
Implementation

Design and implementation

I'm moving the approved direction to a working, responsive site. I code views, run contact forms and take care of SEO, speed and convenient use on mobile devices.

05
Start online

Publication and promotion

I launch the website, configure the domain, hosting, SSL and SEO settings. Depending on the selected scope, the whole thing may be complemented by an advertising spot or a Google Ads campaign.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Specific answers before starting cooperation: costs, deadlines, materials, SEO, publication and post-implementation support.

How long does the project take?

I set the exact date before starting cooperation. Standard:

  • Landing page - up to 5 business days
  • Company website with subpages - 7-14 business days
Do I need to have a ready logo and visual identification?

NO. If the company does not yet have a coherent image, I can prepare a logo, choose the colors, typography and visual direction of the website and promotional materials.

Do I have to provide ready-made texts for the website?

NO. I will help organize the offer and prepare content tailored to the company, its clients and the purpose of the website.

Do you take photos and recordings at the client's place?

In the case of selected projects, it is possible to travel and prepare photos or short recordings on site. I operate mainly in Rzeszów and Podkarpacie, and by prior arrangement also in Kraków and Warsaw.

Can I order just the website?

Yes. You can choose one service or decide on a comprehensive implementation including branding, content, visual materials, website and promotion.

Do you create advertising spots and videos?

Yes. I create short advertising spots, animations and AI video materials promoting a company, product, service, facility or a new website.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. I help in selecting, purchasing and configuring a domain and hosting. The first year of both services is included in the website price, so you get a ready-to-run project without additional formalities.

Will my website be visible on Google?

Each page receives the correct structure, technical optimization, speed of operation and Google indexing configuration. Visibility develops gradually and also depends on competition, content and further SEO activities.

Can I count on support and subsequent changes after publication?

Yes. After launching the website, you can contact me regarding maintenance, content updates, photo exchange, new sections or further development of the project.

What does it look like to start cooperation?

It all starts with a short conversation during which I learn about the needs of your company and determine the scope of the project.

  • After accepting the offer, we sign the contract and pay a 30% deposit.
  • I implement the agreed scope and we stay in touch at subsequent stages.
  • After accepting the final version, you pay the remaining 70%.
  • Then I launch the website under your domain.
