Privacy Policy

Update date: June 2026

1. General information

This Privacy Policy sets out the rules for the processing of personal data of users of the Harvo Studio website.

The administrator of personal data is Karolina Harpak, owner of Harvo Studio.

Contact in matters related to personal data protection: kontakt@harvostudio.pl.

2. Scope of collected data

When using the website, the following data may be collected:

  • name and surname,
  • email address,
  • phone number,
  • content of the message sent via the contact form.

Providing data is voluntary, but necessary to answer the submitted inquiry.

3. Purpose of data processing

Personal data is processed solely for the purpose of:

  • replying to a message sent via the contact form,
  • customer contact,
  • preparing an offer or providing services related to website design.

4. Legal basis for processing

The data is processed on the basis of:

  • art. 6 section 1 letter a GDPR - user consent,
  • art. 6 section 1 letter b GDPR - activities aimed at concluding a contract or providing a service.

5. Data storage period

Personal data is stored for the period necessary to achieve the purpose of contact or cooperation and for the time required by law.

6. User rights

Each data subject has the right to:

  • access to your data,
  • rectification of data,
  • deletion of data,
  • processing restrictions,
  • object to processing,
  • data transfer,
  • submit a complaint to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office.

7. Cookies

The website may use cookies to ensure proper operation of the website and to improve the comfort of using the website.

The user can manage cookies independently through the settings of his web browser.

8. Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold or made available to third parties, except for cases required by law or entities supporting the operation of the website and e-mail.

9. Changes to the privacy policy

The Administrator reserves the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy. The current version of the document is always available on the Harvo Studio website.
